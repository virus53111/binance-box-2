NEXUS ALPHA ENGINE

Solana token scanner, CEX listing watch and market regime in one live radar

Early token intelligence. NEXUS ALPHA monitors new Solana and Pump.fun tokens, DEX liquidity, market cap, volume, buy/sell flow, holder concentration, mint/freeze authority, creator history and security flags.

Listing intelligence. The radar checks major exchange symbol universes and official listing/roadmap sources to surface PRE-CEX evidence while clearly separating ticker matches from contract verification.

Accumulation & breakout setups. Flow heuristics combine turnover versus liquidity, price compression, buy pressure and liquidity changes. They are research signals, not guaranteed forecasts.

Market context. BTC trend, SOL relative strength and volatility are combined into a live bull, bear or flat market regime with risk-on/risk-off context.

Radar Score — приоритизация для исследования, а не прогноз доходности и не рекомендация купить. Микрокап-токены могут потерять всю стоимость. Проверяйте контракт и источники самостоятельно.
